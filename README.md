🌟 Featured AI & Engineering Project

🤖 AI Due Diligence Copilot (Privacy-First Local LLM)

An enterprise-grade, privacy-first AI assistant designed to accelerate financial due diligence. Built for private equity, VC, and M&A analysts, this tool ingests financial documents, processes them entirely locally to maintain strict data privacy, and generates structured boardroom-ready reports.

Tech Stack: Python, Streamlit, Ollama (Local AI), PyMuPDF4LLM

Key Features:

100% Local Processing: Zero data leakage. Documents never leave the host machine.

Financial Table Preservation: Accurately converts complex balance sheets and income statements into LLM-readable Markdown.

Context Overlap Chunking: Custom logic to feed massive documents into local LLMs without dropping data mid-sentence.

Traceable Assertions: Generates 10-part reports (Executive Summary, Risk Assessment, Red Flags) with mandatory source page citations and confidence scores.

1️⃣ Full-Scale Enterprise Sales Dashboard

🎯 End-to-end Power BI project turning raw data into executive intelligence.

The Dataset: 7,991 sales orders across 3 years for a New Zealand-based company selling across Wholesale, Distributor, and Export channels.
The Goal: Turn raw Excel data into a 9-page interactive business intelligence dashboard for executive decision-making.

Data Preparation & Modeling: Cleaned 4 interconnected tables (Star Schema) with zero missing values. Utilized Power Query for transformations and established a dedicated DateTable.

Advanced DAX: Authored 20+ DAX measures including YoY/MoM growth (SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR), Delay Rate % (uncovered ~48% missed targets), Customer LTV, and Margin calculations.

Interactive Dashboard (9 Pages):

Executive Overview (KPIs + Trends)

Sales Trends (Seasonality)

Customer Insights (Segmentation & Top 10)

Channel Performance

Product Performance (Scatter plot: Price vs Margin vs Volume)

Warehouse Fulfillment

Regional Analysis (Geospatial mapping)

Cost & Profitability

Currency View Breakdown
